Why voice AI, why now

Your customers still pick up the phone. Your team can't scale to meet them.

Every enterprise runs the same equation. Call volumes grow. Hiring doesn't keep pace. Simple queries block agents from complex ones. Outbound campaigns get cut because there aren't enough seats to run them.

Legacy IVRs frustrate customers into hitting zero. Chat bots deflect but don't resolve. And most AI voice tools are demo-grade — they hold a nice conversation, but they can't actually update a CRM record, book a slot, take a payment or escalate to the right human when it matters.

CLARA is built for the third option: conversations that finish the job.
Platform overview

An AI voice agent that thinks in context and works in your stack

CLARA joins a call — inbound or outbound — with everything it needs to be useful. Customer history from your CRM. Business rules from your ops team. Live access to your calendars, payment systems, ticketing tools and knowledge base. When the conversation calls for action, CLARA takes it. When it calls for a human, CLARA hands off with the full context intact.

01 · Listen

Listen and understand

Real-time speech recognition tuned for accents, noise and code-mixed languages including Hindi, Hinglish and regional Indian languages. Interruption-aware, so the conversation feels natural.

02 · Reason

Reason with context

CLARA reads live customer data before it speaks — plan details, past interactions, outstanding balance, appointment history — and follows the business rules you've set. No hallucinated policies, no off-script promises.

03 · Act

Complete the action

Update the CRM. Book the appointment. Take the payment. File the ticket. Send the SMS confirmation. Escalate to a human agent with the full transcript. The conversation ends with the work done, not with a follow-up email in someone's queue.

Why CLARA

CLARA reads live data from your CRM, billing and support systems before every conversation. That means it can talk about this customer's plan, this account's balance, this ticket's status — not a generic script. Rules stay yours; CLARA follows them.

Actions, not just answers

Most voice AI tools stop at the conversation. CLARA finishes the work — updates records, books slots, takes payments, files tickets, sends confirmations, escalates with context. The call ends when the job is done.
