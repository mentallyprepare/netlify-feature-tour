How I actually work

Understand · Decide · Act · Follow through

A conversation is only the beginning. Grounded in your data, connected to your systems, honest about what I don't know.

01 · Understand

I listen before I solve

Sub-second endpointing, real barge-in, silence-aware turn-taking. I don't cut anyone off mid-sentence — including in Hinglish and code-switched calls.

02 · Decide

I ground answers in your data

I read your documents, call your APIs, and look up your customer in your CRM before I answer. When I'm not sure, I say so instead of guessing.

03 · Act

I complete the work

Update the CRM. Book the slot. Take the payment. File the ticket. Send the confirmation. The call ends when the job is done — not with a follow-up email in a queue.

04 · Follow through

I hand off cleanly

When a person needs a person, I connect them with the full transcript intact. And ClaraLens grades the whole conversation — mine or a human's — the same way.

Questions people ask about me

FAQ

Short, honest answers — the same ones I give when an answer engine or a buyer asks.

What is Clara?+

Clara is Claritel's agentic AI for business conversations. I take care of voice calls, WhatsApp and website chat — understanding what someone needs, taking action across your CRM and systems, and handing off to a human when one is needed. I have three products: Clara Voice, Clara Chat, and ClaraLens for quality evaluation.

How is Clara different from a chatbot or an IVR?+

A chatbot answers. An IVR asks you to press 1. I act. I understand context from live customer data, follow the business rules your team has set, complete the action on your systems, and follow through. No press-1 trees, no scripted deflection, no telling the customer to send an email.

Does Clara speak Hindi, Hinglish and other Indian languages?+

Yes. I handle English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali and a dozen more languages and dialects — including mid-conversation code switching. Speech models are tuned on real Indian call-centre audio, not clean studio recordings, so accent handling holds up in the field.

Who uses Clara?+

Teams including Panasonic, Rupeek, Zolve, Quick Heal, Atlantic, Univariety and Flowstack use me across sales qualification, customer service, appointments, renewals, feedback and collections. See the customer section above for what each team has me handle.

Is Clara secure and compliant for BFSI?+

Yes. I run on India-resident infrastructure with ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II controls, DPDP-ready processing, TRAI TCCCPR alignment, DLT registration where applicable, PII redaction on transcripts, and role-based access on every recording. ClaraLens gives you an append-only audit trail with evidence anchored to the exact transcript line.

Can I talk to Clara without signing up?+

Yes. Start a live conversation with me from the homepage — no signup, no calendar invite. Two minutes, your number stays private, you can end the call at any time.

How does Clara connect to our CRM and systems?+

I integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, LeadSquared, Zendesk, Freshdesk, ServiceNow, Google Calendar, Outlook, Razorpay, Stripe, WhatsApp Business, SAP and Oracle out of the box — and with anything else through custom API functions and webhooks. If it has an API, I can work with it.

How is Clara priced?+

Usage-based. Per connected minute for voice, per conversation for web chat, per message for WhatsApp, per evaluated interaction for ClaraLens. Prepaid wallet in ₹, UPI or net-banking top-up, GST invoice, no seat licenses, no annual lock-in.

Who built Clara?+

Claritel — an Indian company building agentic AI and cloud telephony infrastructure, headquartered in Bengaluru. Clara is our agentic AI family; the underlying voice runs on Claritel's own enterprise telephony network.
