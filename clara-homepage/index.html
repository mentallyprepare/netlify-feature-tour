Questions people ask

FAQ

Short, honest answers — the same ones I give when an answer engine or a buyer asks.

What is Clara? +

Clara is Claritel's agentic AI for business conversations. I take care of voice, WhatsApp and website chat — understanding what someone needs, taking action across your systems, and handing off to a person when one is needed. I have three products: Clara Voice, Clara Chat, and ClaraLens.

How is Clara different from a chatbot or IVR? +

A chatbot answers. An IVR asks you to press 1. I act. I understand context from live customer data, follow the business rules your team has set, complete the action on your systems, and follow through.

Does Clara speak Hindi, Hinglish and other Indian languages? +

Yes. I handle English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali and other Indian languages — including mid-conversation code switching. Speech models are tuned on real Indian call-centre audio.

Who uses Clara? +

Teams including Panasonic, Rupeek, Zolve, Quick Heal, Atlantic, Univariety and Flowstack use me across sales qualification, customer service, appointments, renewals, feedback and collections.

Can I try Clara without signing up? +

Yes — test an agent free in your browser. Five minutes from empty workspace to a real call.

How is Clara priced? +

Usage-based. Per connected minute for voice, per conversation for chat, per message for WhatsApp, per evaluated interaction for ClaraLens. Prepaid wallet in ₹, UPI or net-banking top-up, GST invoice, no seat licenses, no annual lock-in.
